# Claims and Limitations

**Audience:** public reviewer / claims reviewer  
**Document type:** claims boundary

## What Day 12 proves

Within the frozen synthetic demonstration and its tested rules:

- required onboarding steps can retain explicit owners;
- dependencies can prevent premature downstream completion;
- active blockers can remain visible;
- required completion evidence can gate `COMPLETE`;
- missing/contradictory required information can route to `REVIEW` rather than being invented;
- evaluated public state can be exported.

## What Day 12 does not prove

This release does not prove or claim:

- faster onboarding;
- improved retention, satisfaction, conversion, or revenue;
- production CRM integration;
- automated client communication;
- confidential or regulated-data readiness;
- universal onboarding criteria;
- replacement of human judgment;
- superiority over other tools or workflows.

The demonstration uses synthetic data and a bounded workflow.
