# Day 12 - Client Onboarding Tracker

**BRAIZ WORKS | UBuildOS - DAY 12 / 30**

A signed client can still have a broken start if ownership, dependencies, blockers, or proof of completion disappear during handoff.

Day 12 makes that handoff inspectable.

## The question

**Can every required onboarding step show who owns it, what it depends on, what is blocking it, and what evidence proves it is actually complete?**

## What to inspect

- **Confirm signed agreement** is complete because its required evidence exists.
- **Schedule kickoff** is complete because its predecessor and evidence are satisfied.
- **Collect access requirements** is blocked because the client has not named a system admin.
- **Prepare initial workspace** stays waiting while its required predecessor is incomplete.
- The exported CSV preserves the visible evaluated state.

## Why this small build matters

The interface is intentionally simple. The proof is not that a tracker can be coded.

**The screen may be simple. The test is the whole path around it.**

UBuildOS is testing whether a bounded business problem can move from requirements to a finished, inspectable, tested, independently checked, recoverable, publicly evidenced release without losing the requirements, evidence, or truth along the way.

## Public claim boundary

This release demonstrates deterministic handoff-state logic with synthetic data. It does **not** claim faster onboarding, improved retention, revenue impact, CRM integration, regulated-data readiness, automated client communication, or replacement of human judgment.
